# Flow 움직임 문장 (여의도)

뒷부분 `Keep the same composition, structures and scale as the start frame. Photorealistic. No text, no letters, no logos.`는 모든 장면 공통.

1. 공원: Slow push-in from high above, moving along the length of the park. The faint translucent runway outline gently glows and fades on the park. Trees sway slightly, cars move on the side roads.
2. 1916 모래밭 활주로: Slow push-in along the dirt runway toward the river. Light wind blows sand across the runway, the river water shimmers, a horse near the hangar shifts.
3. 1922 복엽기와 군중: Static camera. The biplane lifts off from the dirt runway and climbs over the river, dust trailing behind it. The crowd in the foreground shifts and raises their heads to watch, hats and clothes moving gently in the wind.
4. 1954 공항: Static camera. Passengers walk slowly across the wet apron toward the parked propeller airliner and climb the boarding stairs, the propellers begin to turn, clouds drift over the control tower.
5. 침수: Slow push-in toward the parked plane. Muddy floodwater flows across the runway, rain falls, small ripples spread around the plane's wheels, workers wade slowly through the water.
6. 빈 활주로: Slow push-in along the empty runway toward the setting sun. Weeds in the cracks sway in the breeze, the sun sinks slowly, construction cranes on the horizon turn slightly.
7. 아스팔트 광장: Static camera. A few cyclists ride slowly across the vast asphalt plaza, heat haze shimmers above the ground, clouds drift slowly over the domed building in the distance.
8. 수송기: Slow push-in toward the green transport aircraft on the lawn. Leaves sway in the breeze, visitors walk slowly along the path, soft clouds drift behind the office towers.
